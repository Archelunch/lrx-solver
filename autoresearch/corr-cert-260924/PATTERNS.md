# Patterns in LP certificates (step 3 input for the symbolic-fit control)

Full tables for m = 4..8 are generated by `python3 patterns.py` into
`PATTERNS_tables.md`: lambda_ab, mu_a(q), and alpha/beta/gamma for the triples
(0,1,2), (0,1,m-1), (1,2,3), (0,m/2,m-1), for three families. The families are
the exact epsilon = 0 certificates, the L1-canonical ones and the L1-canonical
symmetric ones. **No closed form is claimed here.** Every item below is an
observation on one LP vertex unless marked as proved or exactly checked.

## Structural facts (exactly checked or proved)

- **Equality order.** E = 0 at p_i = -i mod m (so K = 0) for every m tested,
  from 4 to 16. It is the unique maximiser up to rotation for m = 4..8 (full
  enumeration). The LP optimum is therefore epsilon >= 0, and every LP run
  reached epsilon = 0 to within 3e-10.
- **Forced tightness.** At the equality order q_ab = m - (b - a). Summing the
  slacks of (5) and (6) there gives Q m (epsilon - E). So in any epsilon = 0
  certificate, every pair condition at q = m - (b - a) is tight. So is every
  triple condition at (q, t) = (m - (b-a), m - (c-b)). The exact
  certificates for m = 4..7 confirm this (max slack 0). A candidate formula
  must satisfy these equalities.
- **Reversal symmetry.** The map a -> m-1-a with positions negated preserves
  E for all orders (checked for m <= 7). kappa_{m-1-b, m-1-a}(q) - kappa_ab(q)
  is a constant d_ab independent of q, and the d_ab sum to 0 (checked for
  m <= 16). A symmetric certificate satisfies:
  - lambda_{m-1-b, m-1-a} = lambda_ab + Q d_ab;
  - mu_{m-1-a}(q) = mu_a(m-q);
  - alpha_{sigma t} = beta_t and gamma_{sigma t} = gamma_t, where
    sigma(a,b,c) = (m-1-c, m-1-b, m-1-a).
  The image of every plain certificate under this map passed the exact check
  with the same epsilon, so the symmetry is compatible. Imposing it costs
  nothing in the LP value.
- **Gauge freedom.** The certificate is not unique. For any constants, the
  shift alpha_abc += s, beta_abc -= s leaves (5) unchanged and moves
  lambda_ab and lambda_bc. mu_a(q) += c_a with lambda compensation also leaves
  (6) unchanged. So "lambda depends only on b - a" is gauge dependent. A fit
  should target gauge-invariant data such as slacks or the P_ab - Q kappa_ab
  profile, or fix a gauge first.

## Observations (one LP vertex each; not formulas)

- **m = 4 needs no triple terms.** A certificate with alpha = beta = gamma = 0
  and integer lambda, mu at Q = 1 has epsilon = 0. There mu_a(q) is linear in q:
  mu_0 = 4(q-3), mu_1 = 4(q-2), mu_2 = -4(q-2), mu_3 = -4(q-1).
- **Exact rational vertices** exist at epsilon = 0 for m = 4, 5, 6, 7 with
  Q = 1, 1, 2, 596 (`certs-exact/`). m = 5 is integer and uses 16 nonzero
  triple coefficients out of 120, with values in {+-5}. m = 6 uses halves and
  39/2. For m >= 8 the float vertices did not rationalise at denominators up to
  5000, with either interior point plus crossover or dual simplex. Either the
  exact vertices have large denominators or HiGHS returned slightly non-basic
  points. Solving the LP exactly is the next step if the fit needs them.
- **Sparsity.** In L1-canonical solutions, roughly 30-35% of triple
  coefficients are nonzero for m = 6..8. About half of the triples carry no
  triple function at all. The zero triples are not the same set across m.
- **Dependence on gaps.** The triple functions do not depend only on the gaps
  (b-a, c-b). The max spread among triples with the same gaps is 5 to 44 at
  Q = 1. mu_a(q) is not linear in q for m >= 5, with residual up to about 28.
  In the chosen gauges lambda_ab is not a function of b - a.
- The m = 6 exact certificate shows the -39/2 blocks in alpha_012 and
  gamma_012 on a q-window of width 2. The m = 5 certificate shows the same
  window shape with -5. This is a possible lead for a window-indicator ansatz
  like [q > b] and [q < m - a] in f_ab. It is untested.

## Suggested fit targets

1. Fix a gauge first, for example mu_a(q) = 0 at q = m - 1 and the symmetric
   family. Then fit the gauge-invariant slack profile.
2. Search an ansatz of window indicators [q > x], [q < m - y] with
   coefficients affine in (a, b, c, m). Enforce the forced-tightness
   equalities as hard constraints.
3. Accept a formula only after `corrcert.check_certificate` passes
   exactly for m = 4..12. Holdout m = 13..20 runs once, per PROTOCOL.md.
