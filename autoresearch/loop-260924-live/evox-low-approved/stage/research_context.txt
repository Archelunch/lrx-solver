LRX Lab research context (development evidence only, frozen 2026-09-24)

Goal and scope
- The general visible sorting-radius conjecture E_r(n) <= T_m(n) is open. This bounded program search studies m=8 families with 4..7 positive zero blocks. A result on these 16 selected development families is not a full m=8 proof.
- The accepted family certificate uses complete L/R/X sorting words on the unit-block state. Trusted replay computes a direct resource profile (base, one slope per retained zero block). An elementary triangle bound controls the direct word's expansion to arbitrary positive block lengths. An exact rational mixture certifies a named family when nonnegative weights sum to 1, weighted base < 31+6k, and each weighted slope <= 6. Finite sample replays guard the implementation; they do not alone prove the all-length statement. A miss or timeout proves no impossibility.

Frozen development baseline

The trusted offline evaluator gives 14/16 certificates, two beyond catalog, with score 2014.0 and zero execution/format failures. Prior findings and claim boundaries
- The supplied nine-gap m=8 theorem covers the nine-positive-block family via a reviewed source argument; it does not close the 4..7-block residual or the full m=8 case. The earlier projection-union global totals remain partly attributed.
- The earlier local JSON mixture-policy campaign added five named all-positive-length family certificates beyond its fixed catalog (four development, one fresh confirmation) on different frozen cases. Its EvoX strategy rewrite did not improve its best. It is evidence that cut/rotation variants can supply complementary profiles, not evidence that an optimizer is superior here.
- The manuscript's stronger lifting candidate A_P <= P+m-2 fails on documented finite r=3 states. This does not refute the sorting-radius conjecture and is not a valid premise for a proof.
- Official GEPA, AdaEvolve, and EvoX integrations passed isolated offline smokes on this same development set. All selected the seed; those tests established execution and trace plumbing, not model performance.
